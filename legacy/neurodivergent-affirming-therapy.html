Neurodivergent Affirming Therapy

I specialize in creating a supportive, understanding environment that respects and celebrates neurodivergent ways of experiencing the world.

You belong here, exactly as you are.

Whether you're navigating an ADHD diagnosis, autism, sensory processing differences, or something else entirely, this is a space designed to honor how your mind actually works — not how the world expects it to.

As a member of the neurodivergent community myself, I understand the exhaustion that comes from constantly adapting, masking, and performing neurotypical expectations. Therapy here is about building strategies that fit your life, your rhythms, and your strengths.

What this looks like in practice:

  • Understanding your own neurodivergent profile and how it shapes your experience.
  • Building practical tools that work with your brain, not against it.
  • Releasing the shame that often comes with being "different."
  • Learning to communicate your needs clearly and confidently.
  • Celebrating your quirks as genuine strengths, not liabilities.
  • Finding community and identity within the neurodivergent experience.

Your brain is not broken.

Let's work together to discover what it's capable of. Book a free 15-minute consultation to get started.
